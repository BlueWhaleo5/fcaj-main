During my internship, I posted 2 blogs to the [AWS Study Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/awsstudygroupfcj):

###  [Blog 1 - The Journey of Amazon SQS](3.1-Blog1/)
The story of Amazon SQS, the very first AWS service to launch globally (2004). Why it was built, how a message queue decouples a system's components, the difference between Standard and FIFO queues, and why it's still core to reliable distributed systems today.

###  [Blog 2 - Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](3.2-Blog2/)
Written after attending the AgentForge - Ho Chi Minh City workshop. An introduction to Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, the platform AWS built to take AI agents from a local demo to a production-grade, enterprise-scale deployment (Runtime, Gateway/MCP, Memory, Identity/Policy/Sandboxing, Observability).
