During my time in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, I had the opportunity to design, build, and deploy **ServerlessFinance**, a serverless financial backtesting and paper-trading engine on AWS, end to end across 8 phases — from the data lake and backtesting core through distributed compute, an API layer, real-time paper trading, cost/performance optimization (including a Rust-accelerated hot path), and CloudWatch observability. Alongside the project, I attended 4 community events (FCAJ Community Day, AgentForge - Ho Chi Minh City, FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week, AgentForge - Deep Dive) and published 2 technical blogs to the AWS Study Group.

To reflect objectively on this period, I evaluate myself against the following criteria:

### Needs Improvement

* Seek out more opportunities for teamwork and cross-functional collaboration, since this project was built independently
* Practice live/verbal presentation of technical work, not just written documentation
* Tighten day-to-day time management within each phase, rather than relying on end-of-phase verification alone
